I Want to Eat Your Pancreas

A story about friendship, memories and the value of time.

About the Story

An introverted high-school boy discovers a diary belonging to his popular classmate, Sakura Yamauchi. Through the diary, he learns that Sakura is living with a serious illness that she has kept secret from most of the people around her.

Although the two seem very different, their unexpected friendship gradually changes both of their lives. As they spend more time together, they begin to appreciate ordinary moments in a completely different way.

The story explores friendship, human connection, memories and the importance of appreciating the time we have with the people around us.
